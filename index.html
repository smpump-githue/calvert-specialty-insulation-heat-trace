Services

What we install

Mechanical Insulation

Insulation systems for energy performance, condensation control, and personnel protection in commercial and institutional buildings.

  • Piping insulation: HVAC, steam, condensate, plumbing, and process
  • Ductwork insulation: duct wrap, rigid board, and closed‑cell
  • Equipment insulation: tanks, pumps, chillers, air separators, and other equipment
  • Metal, PVC, and cloth jacketing
  • Weatherproof insulation for exposed and outdoor systems
  • Vapor barriers, mastics, and finishes

Heat Tracing

Freeze protection for piping and equipment, and temperature maintenance for water, process and drain lines.

  • Self-regulating electric heat trace cable for freeze protection
  • Process temperature maintenance
  • Grease waste and kitchen drain lines, kept warm so grease does not congeal and clog the line
  • Heat tracing for piping, tanks, and equipment
  • Controls and system labeling
  • Cable resistance testing before and after insulation, with documented results
